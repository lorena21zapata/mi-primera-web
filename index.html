About me

I'm a translation student, interested in the intersection of language, technology and digital communication. My academic journey has allowed me to explore translation, foreign languages, teaching methodologies and intercultural communication. I'm particularly interested in localization and how digital products can be adapted to different linguistic and cultural contexts. Alongside my linguistic background, I'm developing my web development skills, exploring how technologies such as HTML, CSS and JavaScript can contribute to creating accessible, functional and user-centered digital experiences. I believe that language and technology complement each other, and I'm interested in building a professional path where both disciplines come together.

Localización
